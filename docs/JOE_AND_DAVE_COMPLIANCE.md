# Joe&dave — branding and open-source compliance

## Product identity

The commercial product name is **Joe&dave**. The Operations Intelligence feature is branded **Joe&dave Operations Intelligence**.

Use “Joe&dave” in product-facing copy, sales materials, and our own product pages. Do not present this repository as an official Frappe or ERPNext product, and do not imply endorsement, sponsorship, certification, or partnership unless written permission exists.

## Upstream identity and notices

This repository is a fork of ERPNext. Keep upstream copyright, attribution, license files, third-party notices, and trademark-policy materials intact. Do not remove or rewrite upstream notices to make the code appear wholly original. Clearly document our modifications and maintain an accurate record of upstream changes.

## GPLv3 distribution checklist

Before distributing a modified ERPNext build or a binary/container containing it:

- Review the repository's actual license files and the current GPLv3 text with qualified counsel.
- Include the applicable license and preserve required copyright and attribution notices.
- Make the complete corresponding source for the covered GPLv3 work available to recipients under the required terms, including our modifications to that work.
- Do not impose additional restrictions that conflict with recipients' GPL rights.
- Audit each separately added dependency/app and its license; do not assume every component has the same license.
- Keep source availability and license notices alongside release artifacts and customer delivery records.

Commercial hosting, implementation, support, and separately licensed services may be possible, but the exact obligations depend on what is distributed and how components are combined. Do not promise that the entire modified ERPNext application can be kept closed-source. Get legal review before customer distribution or signing licensing terms.

## Trademark and name checks

- Do not use ERPNext or Frappe as the Joe&dave product, company, or domain name, and do not use upstream logos as our own brand.
- Factual compatibility wording may be used carefully, for example: “Joe&dave is built on a modified ERPNext codebase.” Avoid wording that implies official affiliation.
- “Joe&dave” is our chosen working brand, not a claim that trademark clearance has been completed. Before public launch, search relevant company, domain, and trademark registers in target markets and obtain legal advice if a conflict appears.
- Use our own logo, color system, screenshots, documentation, and product copy. Keep any required third-party attribution separate and accurate.

## Release gate

No production release until the maintainer has checked the applicable licenses, attribution, source-offer/source-publication process, third-party dependency licenses, trademark clearance, security, tenant isolation, backups, upgrade procedure, and support commitments.

This document is an engineering checklist, not legal advice or a guarantee of compliance.
